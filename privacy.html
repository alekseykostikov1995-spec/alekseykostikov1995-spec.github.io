Villa Salvatore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Developer: AV Games Interactive. Contact: avgamesinteractive@gmail.com.

Villa Salvatore is a puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Game progress

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, estate) is stored only on your device. If you use the “progress code” in Settings, the code is created on your device and you decide where to paste it.

2. Game analytics

To improve difficulty and find crashes, the game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example “level 12 started / won / failed”, booster used, purchase made) together with a random game ID, app version, device model, memory size, OS version, language and short technical error reports (crash logs). The random ID is created by the game and is not linked to your name or accounts. Your IP address is used to determine your approximate country and city. Data is processed by PostHog Inc. on servers in the European Union (PostHog privacy) and kept for up to 12 months.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first (Google consent form); you can change your choice at any time. Learn more: How Google uses data. On iPhone and iPad we ask your permission before using the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). Buying “Remove ads” turns off regular ads.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card details; we only receive confirmation that a product was bought.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows local reminders (daily reward, estate task). They are scheduled on the device; no push server and no personal data are used. You can turn them off in Settings.

6. Legal basis (EU/UK users)

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent. Gameplay analytics and crash reports are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and balanced. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. How to exercise your rights

You can reset the advertising ID in your phone settings, withdraw ad consent, turn off notifications, and delete all game data by uninstalling the game. To request deletion of analytics data, e-mail us with the game ID shown in Settings.

9. Changes

We will update this page if the game starts processing data differently.
