Villa Salvatore — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026. Previous version: 1 November 2026.

Short version. Villa Salvatore has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. Your progress stays on your phone. We use anonymous game statistics and crash reports (PostHog, servers in Germany), ads from Google AdMob and payments through Google Play. We do not sell your data. You can change ad choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings and ask us to delete your data by e-mail with the Game ID from Settings.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

The game Villa Salvatore (the “Game”) is developed and published by AV Games Interactive (Alexey Kostikov, individual developer), Tashkent, Uzbekistan (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy (also called the “personal information processor”, “data owner and operator” or “data controller” in some laws).

Privacy contact, data protection officer and person responsible for personal data: avgamesinteractive@gmail.com. Please include the Game ID shown at the bottom of the Game’s Settings so that we can find your data.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the Game on Android and, if released, on iOS, and to this web page. It does not apply to Google Play, the App Store or Google’s own services, which have their own privacy policies.

3. What data we process

3.1 Data stored only on your device

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, estate upgrades, purchases unlocked), settings (language, sound, reminders) and the random Game ID. This data is not sent to us, except as described below. If you use “Copy progress code”, the code is created on your device and you decide where to paste it.

3.2 Data collected automatically when you play

  • Identifiers: a random Game ID created by the Game on first launch (not linked to your name, Google account or phone number); a random session ID; the advertising ID of your device (used by Google AdMob, see section 6).
  • Gameplay events: level started / won / failed, time and attempts, boosters used, estate upgrades, rooms entered, offers and store screens viewed, purchases made (product and price), ads watched, notification permission choice, game language, level reached, number of coins and stars.
  • Device and app data: operating system and version, device model and year class, memory size, app version.
  • Crash reports: error type, a short error message and the place in the code where it happened. They do not contain your content.
  • Network data: IP address, which every device sends with an internet request. It is used to estimate an approximate country and city and to deliver ads. We do not use it to identify you.

3.3 Data from our partners

  • Google Play / App Store: confirmation that a purchase or subscription was paid, the product, order status and purchase token. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Google AdMob: whether an ad was shown or a reward video was completed, and your consent choice.

3.4 Data you give us

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you.

3.5 Data we do not collect

Name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, precise (GPS) location, health, biometric or other sensitive data. The Game blocks Android permissions it does not need. We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.

6. Advertising, your ad choices and SDKs

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, full-screen ads between levels and optional videos for a reward. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Game shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings; the button appears where Google requires such a choice (including, where enabled, US states with privacy laws).

You can also: reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation on your phone (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking); buy “Remove ads” (turns off banners and between-level ads). If an iOS version is released, it will ask your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier is used.

Software components (SDKs) in the Game that send data: Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google User Messaging Platform (ads and consent), PostHog event API (statistics, called directly by the Game), Google Play Billing (payments). The Game does not use cookies; it stores its settings on your device.

7. Purchases and the VIP subscription

All payments are processed by Google Play (or the App Store). We never see or store your card or bank details. The VIP subscription renews automatically until you cancel it; you can manage or cancel it in Google Play (the “Manage subscription” button in the Game’s Settings opens the store page). Refunds follow the rules of the store.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, a daily reward). No push server is used and no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in the Game’s Settings or in your phone settings.

9. International transfers

We are based in Uzbekistan. Statistics are stored in Germany (EU). Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers are protected by: the adequacy of the EU; Google’s participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission included in Google’s and PostHog’s data processing terms; and the Cabinet of Ministers of Uzbekistan list of countries with adequate protection (Germany; the United States for Data Privacy Framework participants). Regional details are in section 15.

11. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The Game has no accounts or passwords; we collect the minimum of data, use a random ID instead of your identity, limit access to our analytics project to the developer and protect saved progress against tampering. No method is 100% secure; if a data breach affecting you happens, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

12. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13 (in South Korea — under 14). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of digital consent in their country (13–16 depending on the country) without the consent of a parent. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the Game, e-mail us with the Game ID and we will delete the data.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: know what data we have and get a copy; correct it; delete it; restrict processing; object to processing based on legitimate interests (including statistics); data portability; withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier processing); not be subject to decisions based only on automated processing; and complain to a data protection authority. We do not treat you differently for using your rights.

How to use them:

  • Delete everything on your device: uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Ads: Settings → Ad privacy settings; reset the advertising ID in phone settings.
  • Statistics and other data: e-mail avgamesinteractive@gmail.com with the Game ID and what you want (copy, deletion, correction, objection). Because we do not know your name, the Game ID is how we find and verify your data. We answer within one month (sooner where the law requires, e.g. 10 working days in Russia, 10 days in Korea, 30 days in Turkey); complex requests may be extended as allowed by law.
  • Data held by Google: myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
  • You may act through an authorised representative; we may ask for proof of authorisation.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the Game starts processing data differently and change the effective date. For material changes we will tell you in the Game before they take effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
